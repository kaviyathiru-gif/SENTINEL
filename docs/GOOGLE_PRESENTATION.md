### Section 05: Automated Emergency Voice Calls & Gmail Reports (3 Mins)

> **Speaker Script:**  
> *"What happens when a critical breach is detected? Email notifications alone are not enough.*  
>  
> *Sentinel integrates an automated voice call dispatcher. When a critical threat hits, the system places a real cellular phone call to Priority 1 Admin. If unanswered within 10 seconds, it automatically fails over to Priority 2 and Priority 3 SOC leads.*  
>  
> *Once the incident is handled or call failover completes, Sentinel automatically compiles an HTML incident receipt along with daily, weekly, and monthly comparison metrics, dispatching it directly to the Admin’s Gmail account."*

* **Live Demo Action:**  
  1. Click the **Simulate Attack** button in the top navigation header[cite: 1].

  3. Click **Answer Call** to simulate resolution[cite: 1].
